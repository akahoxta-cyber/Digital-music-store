introduction

I Nikolas Hockenjos (akaHoxta) am a Electronic Music Producer  and live DJ from Highbury and Islington, London, making all difrent styles of Dance Elecronica music and has has had a passion for music since he was very little. I put on my first club night at the Lockside  lounge (H.Q's Bar) in Camden London and Deejayed there when I was about 15  years old .
